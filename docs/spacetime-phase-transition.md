# Spacetime Phase Transition

## Motivation

A finite-curvature core can remain within ordinary metric geometry. A stronger possibility is that the metric ceases to be the appropriate microscopic variable before the classical singularity is reached.

The proposed sequence is

\[
\text{semiclassical GR}
\rightarrow
\text{screened geometric regime}
\rightarrow
\text{non-geometric quantum regime}
\rightarrow
\text{emergent geometry}.
\]

This is a hypothesis, not an established result.

## Geometric subspace

A useful abstract picture is

\[
\mathcal H_{\rm fundamental}
\supset
\mathcal H_{\rm geometric}.
\]

States in \(\mathcal H_{\rm geometric}\) admit an approximate map

\[
|\Psi\rangle
\mapsto
(M,g_{\mu\nu},\Phi,\ldots),
\]

while more strongly quantum states need not admit a single classical metric interpretation.

In this language, a black-hole "singularity" could signal failure of the map rather than destruction of the underlying quantum state.

## Why this may be needed

Suppose both spatial infinity and the deep core are nearly flat:

\[
\mathcal K\to0
\]

in both limits.

If the theory is described by a single-valued response function depending only on \(\mathcal K\), it has difficulty distinguishing:

- ordinary weak-curvature vacuum at infinity;
- a post-screening or quantum core with qualitatively different physics.

An additional state variable can distinguish them even when the local curvature is similar.

## Order parameter

Introduce \(\chi\) as a phase label. The effective state is then described by

\[
(g_{\mu\nu},\chi).
\]

For example:

\[
\chi=0
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\text{ordinary geometric phase},
\]

while

\[
\chi\neq0
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\text{screened/core phase}.
\]

The key requirement is not the specific scalar model, but the existence of a dynamically determined variable that carries memory of which branch the system occupies.

## Emergence constraints

Any non-geometric completion must explain how the following emerge:

- Lorentzian signature;
- causal order;
- approximate locality;
- a metric and connection;
- Einstein dynamics at low curvature;
- quantum fields on the emergent background;
- conserved charges such as ADM mass and angular momentum.

Saying "spacetime disappears" without recovering these structures is not a solution.

## Relation to quantum information

Holographic research provides concrete examples in which bulk geometric data are related to entanglement structure in a non-gravitational quantum description. This motivates, but does not prove, the broader idea that geometry can be emergent.

A foundational reference is:

M. Van Raamsdonk, *Building up spacetime with quantum entanglement*, Gen. Rel. Grav. 42, 2323–2329 (2010), arXiv:1005.3035.

The project will not assume that entanglement alone is the microscopic variable. Candidate structures include operator algebras, quantum error-correcting codes, causal networks, tensor networks, and other nonperturbative degrees of freedom.

## Falsification criterion

The phase-transition extension is unnecessary if a single, covariant, stable geometric theory can:

1. produce collapse from regular initial data;
2. resolve the singularity;
3. avoid fatal inner-horizon instability;
4. evaporate consistently;
5. remain unitary.

In that case, the simpler geometric completion should be preferred.
